I teach at the University of Connecticut at the undergraduate, MA, and PhD levels. In 2019 I received the Grillo Family Award for Excellence in Teaching.

Courses
======

* **Health Economics** — taught at the undergraduate, MA, and PhD levels
* **Advanced Labor Economics** — PhD level
* **Intermediate Microeconomics**
* **Quantitative Intermediate Microeconomics**
* **Economic Behavior and Health Policy**

Graduate Advising
======

I have chaired or served on dissertation committees for many PhD students at UConn, with graduates placed in tenure-track academic positions, postdocs, government agencies, and industry. If you are a current UConn PhD student interested in working with me on topics in labor, health, or public economics, feel free to reach out by [email](mailto:david.simon@uconn.edu).
