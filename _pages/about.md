I am an Associate Professor of Economics at the [University of Connecticut](https://econ.uconn.edu/) and a Research Associate at the [National Bureau of Economic Research (NBER)](https://www.nber.org/people/david_simon) in the Program on Children and Families.

My research fields are **labor economics, health economics, public policy, and applied econometrics**. My work focuses on the economics of children and families, the intergenerational transmission of human capital, and applied policy evaluation — including studies of the Earned Income Tax Credit, the social safety net, child welfare policy, and early-life determinants of child health and achievement.

My research has been published in journals including the *American Economic Journal: Economic Policy*, *American Economic Journal: Applied Economics*, *Journal of Public Economics*, *Journal of Human Resources*, *Journal of Health Economics*, and the *Journal of Law and Economics*. My paper "Income, the Earned Income Tax Credit, and Infant Health" (with Hilary Hoynes and Doug Miller) won the AEJ: Economic Policy Best Paper Award in 2018.
